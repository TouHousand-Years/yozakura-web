# Tarot Cards — SeeOne

Source: https://seeone.itch.io/tarot-cards

The 12 tarot face scenes and initial card-back scene in `public/art/scenes/divination/`
incorporate the corresponding 51×79 Original/1X Major Arcana cards and card back
from the free pack by SeeOne. Card colors are
mapped one-to-one to the game's four grayscale colors, and the cards are flattened
onto an AI-generated wooden tabletop. `table.bmp` contains only that tabletop.

The source page permits use in free and commercial projects and modification to
suit the project; credit is appreciated but not required. It prohibits redistributing
or reselling the asset pack. The original ZIP and standalone card PNGs are not
included in this repository. These credits do not grant separate asset-pack rights.

Dimensions, exact source color mapping, generation prompt, automatic perfectPixel
conversion and reproduction script are recorded under `.scratch/divination-backgrounds/`.
